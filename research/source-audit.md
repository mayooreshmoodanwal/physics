The CBSE syllabus states that NCERT content excluded for 2026–27 will not be assessed. Its **16 marks** are shared by Units I and II; there is no published fixed mark allocation for Unit I or either of its chapters. The sample paper has 70 marks, 3 hours, 33 questions and five sections. These are paper-level facts, not Unit I score promises.

**Potential trap:** “Conductors and insulators” remain in the current Chapter 2 syllabus. Excluding the Chapter 1 earthing passage does **not** exclude the Chapter 2 treatment of conductors and insulators.

## Exam-question provenance

The Unit I course links to the **2026–27 CBSE sample paper** Q1 (flux), Q16 (conductor in electrostatic equilibrium), Q19 (potential/work), and Q31 (capacitance or potential gradient). These are **SQP questions, not PYQs**. No question is labelled “official PYQ” until a specific official board paper and marking scheme have been ingested, checked against 2026–27 scope, and recorded by year, set, page and question number.

## Practical boundary

The current CBSE practical list has no Unit I electrostatics experiment. Section B activity 1 includes **identifying a capacitor among components**; it can be cross-linked as a practical connection in Chapter 2, but it is not an electrostatics experiment or a substitute for the listed practical syllabus.
